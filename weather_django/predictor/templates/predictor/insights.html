Geometric & Distance Mechanics

Mathematical impact of feature standardization and distance calculations.

1.

Z-Score Feature Standardization

z_j = (x_j - μ_j) / σ_j

Atmospheric pressure (~1015 hPa) has variance on the order of 100, while precipitation (0-20 mm) has variance under 15. Without standardization, the distance calculation would be 98% dominated by pressure alone. Standardization brings all features to mean 0 and unit variance, granting each meteorological sensor equal geometric influence.

2.

Distance Metrics Evaluated

Euclidean (L2): d(x, y) = √(∑ (x_i - y_i)²)
Manhattan (L1): d(x, y) = ∑ |x_i - y_i|

Manhattan distance measures grid-like rectilinear distances across the 22 standardized dimensions, which proved slightly more robust against multidimensional outliers in this dataset (96.67% accuracy vs 95.42% for Euclidean).

3.

Neighborhood Plurality Voting

For any query vector, the distances to all 960 training samples are computed. The K={{ best_k }} lowest distance samples are extracted, and their categorical labels (Sunny, Rainy, Cloudy, Snowy) cast equal votes.
